Why aren't these in the Connection Manager?

The Connection Manager is designed for Marketing users to configure Activation Channels — where customer data is sent for campaigns (Webhooks, Email, Push, SMS). Mautic and the Data Warehouse are CDP infrastructure: they power the backend engines that Marketing users interact with through the Audiences and Data modules. Exposing their raw config to Marketing users would violate the platform's "hide backend complexity" principle.
